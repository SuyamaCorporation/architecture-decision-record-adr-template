Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Template

Document software architecture decisions in a structured format

ADR Header

Context

What is the issue we are seeing that motivates this decision? Describe the forces at play—technical, political, social, project constraints.

Decision Drivers

What are the key concerns, constraints, or quality attributes that must be satisfied?

Options Considered

List the alternatives evaluated, with their pros and cons.

Decision

The change we decided to make. State the decision clearly and the rationale.

Consequences

✅ Positive Consequences

⚠️ Negative Consequences / Trade-offs

Implementation Notes

Any notes, links, migration steps, or follow-up tasks related to implementing this decision.
